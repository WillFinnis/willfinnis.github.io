ROVER

The Autonomous Garden Care Robot

A modular robot that can assist and complete the more monotonous and time-consuming activities of garden care and maintenance, freeing up the users' time and allowing them to focus on their favourite gardening activities. This helps nurture better physical and mental health in an affordable, environmentally conscious and sustainable way.

Hands Free Hair Dryer

Rheumatoid Arthritis ultimately results in the wearing away of cartilage between the bones in a joint. The reduction of this smooth surface means the bones rub against each other causing joint damage and pain. This assistive hair dryer reduces the stress felt by ones joints while drying their hair by completely removing the weight of the hair dryer and the need to extend ones arms up and around ones head which can cause pain and discomfort.

Safety Bike Light

Cycling in heavily populated areas can be dangerous, especially at night. This bike light not only allows cars to clearly see the bike but also to clearly see the behaviour of the bike, such as whether it is turning or slowing down. Therefore, making a much safer and enjoyable journy for the cyclist.
