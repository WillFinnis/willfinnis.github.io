About Me

I’m a creative and technically driven Electronics Engineer with hands-on experience across mechanical, electronic, and software integration projects. I recently graduated from Loughborough University with a 2:1 in Product Design & Technology and a 1st in my final year project that achieved an innovative design commendation from Jaguar Land Rover. I have developed strong design, prototyping, and problem-solving skills from working on a diverse range of projects including robotic lawn mowers at Bosch, classic car restorations and marine engineering. I’m skilled in circuit design, CAD software, prototyping, manufacturing, testing, ensuring compliance to safety standards, and have practical real-world project experience working collaboratively in multidisciplinary, multinational teams with tight deadlines. I’m passionate about improving people’s lives by creating innovative, well-crafted products that combine technical precision with practical design.
